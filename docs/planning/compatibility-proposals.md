# Compatibility proposals for 2.0

**Revision notice2026-10-17:** [modern direction](modern-direction-review.md#how-this-revises-the-earlier-draft) proposes replacing C03 and the travel/entry-exhaustion portion of C02 with [transport](transport-proposal.md). Do not combine the old170-point residue formula with the proposed13-node transport model. Other rows remain recommendations for separate review; nothing here is approved by the newer direction alone.

Drafted 2026-10-17. **PROPOSAL — unapproved.** These recommendations turn the [original research handoff](original-research-handoff.md) into reviewable choices. They are not new findings, agreed rules, or permission to start application implementation. The original audit remains the record of shipped behaviour.

The recommended approach is to preserve the original economic and action structure, then make a small, explicit set of consistency improvements. “Keep” below can deliberately preserve an implementation quirk; “change” describes a proposed departure even when the change looks like a bug fix. New names and illustrations are reskins only while their rule roles remain unchanged.

“Review first” identifies choices that affect the eventual simulation contract. “Later” means the preservation recommendation can wait for the content/balance review; it does not mean it has already been accepted. Validation scenarios below are proposed checks for a future implementation, not tests already run or claims of observed original gameplay.

## Suggested review order

First agree the edition and the small set of departures that affect every action or save: **C01–C06, C08, C10 and C15–C17**. Then settle grouped possessions and used stock together (**C13–C14**). The deliberately retained economy, loan-refusal and durable-reward quirks (**C07, C09, C11–C12**) deserve a conscious acknowledgement, rather than an assumption that “nearly identical” automatically answers them.

These proposals do not claim the combined balance will match the original. Money/score consistency, fair-information AI and loan/garnishment changes can alter success rates even with identical content. A future validation pass should compare a few fixed opening, setback and recovery scenarios before introducing new careers, courses or rewards. No additional meter, subscription system, real-world qualification claim, online leaderboard or backend is proposed here.
