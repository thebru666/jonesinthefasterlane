## Which employers could change the town?

These are alternatives, not a recommendation to add all of them. Keep the existing essential housing, rent, food, clothing, learning, finance and used-goods functions. First select one or two career identities; then decide which current employer they replace or share a destination with. A shared facade does not make a second employer free of content/balance work.

| Candidate | Example game roles | What makes visiting it interesting | Scope and recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Café and proper restaurant** | Kitchen hand, cook, chef, venue manager | A visible step up from entry-level food work; restaurant recruits a dependable cook | Strongest fit for the owner's offer example. Explore a hospitality destination with a café and a restaurant employer, or upgrade the current café ladder. A second employer means new records, not a reused vacancy. |
| **Courier and parcel depot** | Sorter, dispatcher, route planner, depot manager | Contemporary local work; good/bad trading periods are understandable | Strong candidate alternative employer. Keep normal work shifts initially; do not silently add delivery driving, vehicle requirements or a route minigame. |
| **Community clinic** | Reception, administration, support, practice manager | Broadens the town beyond shops and manufacturing; offers a distinct character and course route | Strong candidate for variety. A new administration/support branch is simpler than a clinical-training simulation. Course/job requirements still need design. |
| **Trades and repair workshop** | Workshop assistant, technician, estimator, business manager | Connects practical learning, repair and useful possessions | Could reshape TownWorks or Reboot rather than add another stop. Keep the appliance, vehicle and industrial roles distinct where their rules differ. |
| **Design and digital studio** | Studio assistant, designer, producer, studio manager | A recognisable home-working career and a reason to want a laptop | Good contrast to physical jobs; requires a new training branch or a carefully reviewed replacement. Avoid a generic instant-cash freelance button. |
| **Cinema and recreation centre** | Usher, events assistant, technician, venue manager | Connects work, outings, humour and entertainment purchases | Excellent personality; consider later or as a replacement employer. Venue employment and leisure tickets remain separate actions. |

**Recommended shortlist:** hospitality plus either clinic or parcel depot. The studio is attractive if home working should be prominent; otherwise one existing office occupation can test that capability without a full new employer. Retain some manufacturing/trades work so the town is not entirely retail and offices. No exact thirteen-stop replacement plan is selected yet.

## Why explore individual businesses?

Normal applications should have the same requirements, time cost and odds at the employer and the civic desk. In-store application already saves a journey when the player is at the workplace. Add a few distinctive opportunities, rather than a permanent invisible “visited the shop” hiring bonus.

| Opportunity | Proposed benefit | Simplicity boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Public listings** | Civic desk compares ordinary town-wide jobs; employer shows its own matching records | Same job, same public offered wage, same turn-level rejection memory. Changing venues does not reroll a failed application. |
| **Local recruitment notice** | Occasionally an existing employer has an unadvertised lead or a clearly labelled higher-pay recruitment offer | One authoritative offer record, not conflicting copies of the same public quote. Show its employer, role, pay, expiry and requirements. Not every visit gives a new roll. |
| **Personal job offer** | Good relevant work history prompts an employer to recruit the player into a specific qualified role | Reuse experience, dependability and job/qualification history where available; no new reputation/loyalty meter. A new cross-employer relevance mapping is needed. |

**Recommended approach:** ordinary hiring remains ordinary; occasional local notices reward exploration; rare personal offers reward being good at the job. Use an explicit wage offer or reserved opening, rather than secretly increasing hire probability. Avoid stacking a wage premium, guaranteed opening and extra rewards on every opportunity.

Local notices are generated on the defined incoming-turn/economy boundary and retained, not generated by opening the board. Store discovered notices for the turn. Revisiting, switching between civic/employer screens, inspecting the phone or reloading cannot create fresh notices. Any quota/expiry tuning is still open. The civic desk should say “Local notices may also be available” so the broader discovery system is understandable without revealing every lead.

### Personal offer example

> “We hear you're dependable on the grill. The Copper Spoon has a cook opening with your name on it. Interested? We even have plates.”

The offer shows **role, pay, requirements, work location and expiry**. Accepting is a choice; it must not automatically quit the current job. Recommend a reserved opening while the offer is valid, with the usual qualifications still required. Keep the ordinary four-time hiring action on acceptance; presentation of the offer itself is free. Switching jobs retains the same downstream employment/clothing/growth rules except for explicitly stated offer overrides. Ordinary applicants still use the ordinary opening rules.

**Proposed expiry:** available through the player's next full turn; state this in player-turn terms, not an ambiguous “this week.” Expire at the start of the following personal turn. Recommend at most one active personal offer per player initially; exact triggers/frequency/pay need tuning. No offer should require buying a qualification before the recipient can meet its stated conditions within its lifetime. A new restaurant and its roles are necessary before this particular invitation exists; it cannot be inferred from the current37-job graph.

Display offers during turn preparation and keep them in a simple Offers view accessible to everyone. Phone ownership is not required to learn about a personal offer. Declining does not punish the player. AI uses the same qualifications, notice discovery, ownership benefits, pay and acceptance costs; it cannot read undiscovered notices.

## Possessions that change a decision

Recommend one clearly explained main benefit per object. Keep food/clothes/transport/work/study as the underlying resources rather than adding battery, data, followers or subscription administration.

| Object | Proposed tangible benefit | Tradeoff / limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Phone** | Refresh current advertised pay and discovered recruitment notices from known employers without revisiting a board | Does not discover all hidden local notices, predict random openings, apply for ordinary jobs or perform work. Everyone still has basic help, Goals and personal-offer access. Prices/expiry are shown accurately; failed-application memory persists. |
| **Laptop** | Existing study-equipment role, plus up to one normal paid work-from-home shift per personal turn in a job explicitly marked eligible | Same six-time shift, ordinary pay/work growth and partial-shift rules; saves commuting, not work time. No remote physical jobs. Requires current employment, not ownership alone. Other shifts can still be worked on site. |
| **Fridge/freezer** | Retained grocery storage/spoilage benefits | Keeps bulk food useful; no separate meal inventory or kitchen simulation. |
| **Cooker or microwave** | Candidate improvement to the existing Relax action when groceries are available: one small extra recovery/Happiness benefit per personal turn | Choose one shared food-preparation benefit; owning both must not multiply it. This would be additional to, or replace, the original recurring ownership effect only by explicit choice. No free second food stockpile. |
| **Console or TV** | Candidate small boost to the first Relax action of the turn | No passive Happiness every render or repeat-click reward loop. Prefer one entertainment category benefit rather than stacking each screen/device. |
| **Study books** | Retain the combined-set study benefit; show the needed set clearly | A cheaper physical alternative alongside the laptop's original equipment role. Do not invent independent credits per book. |
| **Bicycle/car** | Agreed direction: save travel time at different financial costs | No automatic career qualification or goal points for owning transport. |

**Recommend specifying phone, laptop and food storage first.** They make concrete, different promises. Then choose whether cooking and entertainment improve Relax; they are candidate alternatives, not five new actions to add at once. Some possessions can simply be enjoyable and visibly furnish the apartment.

Work-from-home eligibility must be an explicit reviewed job attribute. An office coordinator is a plausible fictional trial candidate; a cook, machine operator or shop-floor technician is not. The prototype must test whether one remote shift leaves transport useful. The new paid remote shift must replace the relevant original random computer-income event for that laptop slot, rather than automatically add two income benefits; retaining or replacing that event is a choice to settle. Study equipment credit remains a separately stated original effect.

**Happiness clarification:** enjoying possessions belongs in the successor's intent. The actual inspected original has18 normal durable-purchase bonus branches that skip their nominal bonuses after receipt; [the audit](../rules-audit/happiness-actions.md#all18-nominal-durable-purchase-branches) documents that quirk. The previous C12 recommendation was to retain it initially. Adding purchase Happiness or device-enhanced Relax now is a conscious balance change, not an existing reward we have proved works. Recommend modest, explicit first-ownership rewards only if selected, with an acquisition-history flag surviving sale/loss so rebuying cannot farm points. Amounts and which objects qualify remain undecided.
